The app

Tonight reads this evening's events on your iPhone, only if you allow it, and shows how many mapped cameras sit within about two blocks of each venue. Nothing leaves your phone.

Map shows the City of Chicago's published speed and red light cameras, and an optional layer of cameras mapped by OpenStreetMap volunteers. Every layer shows its source and date. Maps can be incomplete.

Pairs holds your frames and their certificates. A demo pair is included.

Common questions

Does Signet collect my face? No. No face scans, no photos of you, no account.

Why does the app want my calendar? Only for Tonight, and only if you tap Allow. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

How do I order a pair or a fitting? Open Pairs, choose a tier in The Collection, and tap Request. Nothing is charged in the app.
